# Code of Conduct

We expect respectful, constructive, and inclusive participation.

- Be considerate and professional.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy and never publish customer data or credentials.
- Welcome good-faith questions and technical disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and doxxing are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community. Report serious incidents privately to the repository owner.
